# Patchsentry community code of conduct

This is Patchsentry's project policy. It applies to project issues, pull requests,
reviews, documentation discussions and other spaces maintained for this project.

We welcome people with different backgrounds, disciplines and levels of experience.
Documentation, reproducibility reports, accessibility reviews, design feedback and
test cases are useful contributions alongside code.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully and explain the reasoning behind feedback.
- Be patient with questions; give newcomers enough context to participate.
- Respect names, pronouns, accessibility needs and personal boundaries.
- Credit contributions accurately and distinguish observed results from assumptions.
- Keep private information, credentials and customer data out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, impersonation and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Do not pressure
someone to disclose personal information or contact them after they ask you to stop.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to **[patchsentry@gmail.com](mailto:patchsentry@gmail.com)**.
Include links or a brief account of what happened and any immediate concerns.
Share only the personal information needed to understand the report. If the
concern involves the maintainer, you may use GitHub's platform reporting tools or
an appropriate institutional contact rather than contacting that person directly.

The maintainer will handle reports as availability permits and limit sharing to
what is needed to address them. Confidentiality and a specific response time cannot
be guaranteed. Depending on the situation, moderation may include clarification,
a request to change behavior, removal of content, a warning or restriction of
participation. Decisions should be proportionate and can be questioned privately.
A conduct report or good-faith disagreement must not be grounds for retaliation.
